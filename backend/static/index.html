Pending Allocation to Vendors

How this page works (click to show / hide)

  1. Find the requests — use the filters below (MAWB, Account, Department, Origin, Initial Call dates). Requests are grouped by Origin, then by Initial Call time window.
  2. Tick the requests that go on one truck (all from the same origin). The green / yellow boxes suggest requests that fit one truck — Select these ticks them for you.
  3. In Route to Vendor (it opens below once something is ticked): set the drop order, choose the Truck Type, then the Vendor — the suggested vendor is picked for you, and you may choose another.
  4. Final Call Datetime is optional — leave it blank if it is not confirmed yet; the trip then shows its Initial Call Datetime (TBC).
  5. Click Allocate to Vendor. The trip moves to Vendor Truck Assignment, where the vendor assigns a driver.

1. Getting started

Top
  1. Open the app link and sign in with your company Google account. There is no separate app password.
  2. The menu on the left only shows the pages your role can use, grouped under Main, Operations, Reports, Administration and Help. On a phone, tap the button (top left) to open it.
  3. Your name, initial and role are shown at the bottom left, under the menu. To sign out, sign out of Google in your browser.
  4. A page you need is missing? Ask a Master Admin — they grant pages per role (topic 17). A brand-new account sees no pages until an admin gives it a role.

2. Dashboard

Top
  1. Optional: set the filters (Final Call From / To, Account, Department, Status, Origin, Destination, MAWB) and click Apply Filters.
  2. The cards show how many requests are in each status. Click a card to list only those requests; click Show all requests to go back.
  3. Available Trucks opens Fleet Management.

3. Creating a truck request

Top
  1. Open New Request. The Booking Date is filled in for you.
  2. Fill the fields marked *: Department, Origin Port, Account, Destination Port (different from the origin), Packaging Type, Quantity and Initial Call datetime (TBC). MAWBs, customs time, weight / volume and instructions are optional.
  3. Click Submit Request. The request appears on the Masterlist and on Pending Allocation as Pending.
  4. Many requests at once: click Download Excel template. Fill one row per request — Department, Origin Port, Account, Destination Port and Packaging Type are drop-down lists from the Master Library, so just pick from them. Row 2 (yellow) is only an example — delete that row before uploading (if it is left in, it is skipped and never created). Choose the file, check the preview and click Create … requests. Rows with problems are explained and skipped — the others are still created.

4. Masterlist

Top
  1. Find requests with the search box and filters. Click a column header to sort; drag a header to move a column (Reset Columns puts them back).
  2. Actions on each row: View (every detail on one screen, with the download links), Edit (status, Revised Qty, milestones, attachments, manifest) and Delete (Pending requests only).
  3. Manifest: a red Missing Manifest means no file yet — click it, then upload the manifest in the Manifest section: an Excel (.xlsx / .xlsm) or CSV (.csv) file, up to 10 MB. The bag / carton IDs are read from it and download as an Excel file (Download Excel).
  4. Once the driver ends a scan, Pickup Scan Manifest and Unloading Scan Manifest download links appear in the Manifest column and in View. Unloading Remarks shows what the driver typed at the drop.
  5. In View, under each scan manifest, the scan counts show how many bags were successful and had no scan, for that request (its own manifest IDs at pickup; the bags picked up for its drop at unloading). Override (not on manifest) appears only when bags that were not on the manifest were accepted with the override code (at pickup) or unloaded at this drop.
  6. Revised Qty is set by whatever happened last: the Qty typed (New Request / Edit), a manifest upload (its number of IDs) or the end of the unloading scan (bags unloaded). Typing a different Qty in Edit after a manifest or unloading scan overrides it. Masterlist, View, Edit and Trip Utilisation all show the same number; View says where it comes from.
  7. Pickup / Unloading Scan Manifest downloads are Excel files: bag IDs show in full and Trip IDs stay as text (e.g. 11E-00087). Override bags show the Trip ID of the drop where they were unloaded (e.g. 11E-00087-A), or the trip ID if not unloaded yet.
  8. An allocated trip with a driver shows Remove driver: the trip goes back to Vendor Truck Assignment.
  9. Export CSV downloads every request that matches your filters, with the same columns as the table.
  10. Master Admin only: Clear All deletes every request in the system — it cannot be undone.
Status flow: Pending → Allocated → In Transit → Delivered. On Hold, Cancelled and Foul Trip are set from Edit (Foul Trip needs a date, a reason and a count of 1 or 2).

5. Allocating requests to vendors

Top
  1. Open Pending Allocation to Vendors. The short How this page works box at the top repeats these steps. Narrow the list with the filters: MAWB, Account, Department, Origin and the Initial Call date range. Requests are grouped by origin port and Initial Call time; each card shows the Account.
  2. Tick the requests that should go on one truck (same origin only), or click Select these on a Consolidation suggestion (green when the truck would be over 85% full).
  3. Step 1 – Drop order: suggested nearest-first from the origin; change the numbers if needed. Drop 1 is the main destination.
  4. Step 2 – Truck type: the Trip Utilisation box shows how full the truck is (red <70%, yellow 70–85%, green >85%).
  5. Step 3 – Vendor: the vendor with the lowest rate for this trip is suggested and pre-selected (no rate amounts are shown). You may pick another vendor.
  6. Step 4 – Final Call datetime is optional: leave it blank if it is not confirmed yet — the trip then shows its Initial Call datetime (TBC) (set the Final Call later in Masterlist → Edit). Click Allocate to Vendor. The requests get a Trip ID and drop numbers and leave this page.
To undo, open the request on the Masterlist, Edit, and set the status back to Pending.

6. Fleet Management

Top
  1. Lists every truck with its type, vendor, driver, phone, current requests and availability. Vendors only see their own trucks.
  2. Admins and vendors: Add Truck — plate number, truck type, vendor, driver name and phone.
  3. Use to edit, to switch a truck between Available and Not Available, and to delete.

7. Vendor Truck Assignment

Top
  1. Shows every trip that still needs a driver, with all its drops and MAWBs, and its Final Call — or its Initial Call datetime (TBC) when no Final Call is set yet.
  2. Filter by Department, Truck Vendor, Truck Type and Origin (vendors see Truck Type and Origin).
  3. Vendors pick a Driver / Fleet account for each trip and click Assign Driver (see the User Guide for Vendors). Admins see the list in view-only mode.
  4. The trip then shows the driver and truck plate on the Masterlist. It stays Allocated until the driver taps Depart in the scanning app.

8. Driver / Fleet Accounts

Top
  1. These are the usernames and passwords drivers use in the Scanning App. Each account drives one truck.
  2. Add Account: username, password (4+ characters), driver name, phone, vendor (admins choose; vendors get their own), the truck, and an Override code (4–32 letters / numbers) used to accept a bag that is not on the manifest.
  3. Edit with (leave the password / code blank to keep them), untick Account is active to switch an account off, or delete with .
  4. The Assigned Trips column shows each account's open trips and how far the driver has got.
Admins are asked for the page password here (topic 18); vendors are not.

9. Scanning App

Top
  1. A phone screen for drivers: open Scanning App from the menu (or the app link ending in #/scan) and sign in with a Driver / Fleet account.
  2. The driver follows the trip steps in order: arrive at pickup → scan bags → end loading → depart → for each drop: arrive, scan bags off, add photos and remarks, finish.
  3. The full step-by-step is in the User Guide for Driver/Fleet — also opened with the Guide button inside the scanning app.

10. Rate Management

Top
  1. Click Add Rate: vendor, truck type, origin, Main Destination, Rate/Trip, Default Rate, Foul Trip %, Fuel Surcharge %, Effective and Expiry dates, and optional Additional Destination Drops with their own rates (a drop cannot be the Main Destination).
  2. How a trip is priced: drop 1 to the Main Destination gets Rate/Trip. Later drops are extra stops on the same trip: their Additional Destination Drop rate if listed, otherwise the Default Rate.
  3. Foul Trip % (foul trips) and Fuel Surcharge % (delivered requests) are worked out on that same rate.
  4. Filter by vendor, origin, destination and truck type. Rates are grouped by route; each group lists the lowest rate per truck type, and the Remark column marks the cheapest vendor.
Asks for the page password when one is set (topic 18).

11. Vendor Speed Performance

Top
  1. Filter by request #, vendor, truck type, plate, origin, destination, account, department, MAWB or Final Call dates. A request with no Final Call yet is matched on its Initial Call (TBC) date, which the Final Call column then shows marked (Initial TBC).
  2. Each row shows the time taken for every leg of the trip (customs to arrival, loading, travel, unloading, full leg). Click a header to rank vendors; Export CSV downloads the table.
Asks for the page password when one is set (topic 18).

12. Cost Analysis

Top
  1. Choose the Final Call From / To dates (required) and any other filters, then click Generate Report. A request with no Final Call yet is included by its Initial Call (TBC) date.
  2. Only Delivered and approved Foul Trip requests are counted. Click Total Requests, Delivered or Foul Trip to list just those.
  3. Actual Cost comes from Rate Management (topic 10). A Foul Trip has no Actual Cost and no Fuel Surcharge — it is charged as the Foul Trip Cost (rate × Foul Trip % × count of 1 or 2).
  4. Admins: edits one request's costs; Bulk Cost Update adds amounts to the requests you tick (split equally, or the full amount to each).
  5. Export CSV downloads the table as shown.
Asks for the page password when one is set (topic 18).

13. Foul Trip Approval

Top
  1. A request becomes a Foul Trip from Masterlist → Edit (date, reason and a count of 1 or 2 are required).
  2. Here, check each foul trip, set its count if needed, then press the green to approve it. Only approved foul trips go into Cost Analysis.
  3. Click Awaiting Approval or Approved to filter the list; Export CSV downloads it.

14. Trip Utilisation Review

Top
  1. Shows delivered trips and how full each truck was: Initial (the quantities when the requests were made) and Revised (each request’s Revised Qty, the same number as the Masterlist).
  2. The Final Call From / To filters use the Initial Call (TBC) date for a trip with no Final Call yet; the Final Call column then shows that date marked (Initial TBC).
  3. Click Green, Yellow or Red Trips to list only those; Export CSV downloads the table.
Asks for the page password when one is set (topic 18).

15. Master Library

Top
  1. The lists used in every dropdown: Ports (click the map to set a port's location), Accounts, Departments, Packaging, Statuses, Truck Types and Vendors.
  2. Truck Types need a Max Capacity for the utilisation %.
  3. Ports and Accounts have Export CSV (same columns as the table on screen).
  4. Capacity Conversion: choose the base unit (e.g. Pallet) and how much of it one unit of each other packaging is worth (e.g. Carton 0.02 = 50 cartons per pallet). Click Save.

16. User Roles

Top
  1. Everyone who has signed in is listed. Change a person's access with the Role dropdown (takes effect when they reload the page).
  2. Roles: Viewer, Normal User, Admin, Master Admin, Driver/Fleet, a Vendor role, or New User (no access). Only a Master Admin can give the Master Admin role.
  3. Click a name to rename it, or to remove the user.

17. Role Visibility (Master Admin)

Top
  1. A table of pages (rows) and roles (columns). Tick what each role may open, then click Save Changes.
  2. Type over a role name at the top of its column to rename how it is shown everywhere.
  3. Driver/Fleet normally has only the Scanning App, so its users go straight into the scanning app.
  4. What you save is exactly what each role gets — nothing is ticked back automatically. If you untick Role Visibility for Master Admin, the app asks you to confirm first.

18. Page Password & Access Logs

Top
  1. One password protects Driver / Fleet Accounts (not for vendors), Rate Management, Cost Analysis, Trip Utilisation Review and Vendor Speed Performance. It is asked every time one of these pages is opened.
  2. The Master Admin sets, changes or resets it under Administration → Page Password & Logs. Until it is set, the pages open without asking.
  3. The User Log records every password change and every page opening (with wrong tries). It cannot be edited or deleted.
After 5 wrong tries the page is blocked for 15 minutes.

19. Quick reference

Top
  1. Statuses: Pending → Allocated → In Transit (after Depart) → Delivered; also On Hold, Cancelled and Foul Trip.
  2. Trip ID = vendor code + number + drop letter, e.g. 11E-00087-A (drop 1), 11E-00087-B (drop 2).
  3. Utilisation colours: red <70%, yellow 70–85%, green >85%.
  4. All times are GMT+8. Distance (KM) appears in red once a request is on a trip.

20. Problems and fixes

Top
  1. A page is missing — your role does not have it; ask a Master Admin.
  2. “No pages available” — your account is still New User; ask an admin to set your role.
  3. A page asks for a password — ask the Master Admin for the page password.
  4. Manifest upload refused — it must be an Excel (.xlsx / .xlsm) or CSV (.csv) file of 10 MB or less, with a bag / carton / gunny / sack ID column.
  5. Attachment refused — up to 8 files and 25 MB per request.
  6. No utilisation % — the truck type has no Max Capacity, or a packaging type has no factor (Master Library).
  7. Cost Analysis is empty — check the Final Call dates (a request with no Final Call is matched on its Initial Call date); only Delivered and approved Foul Trip requests count.
  8. Anything else — note the exact message on screen and the page, and send it to the administrators.

1. What you can see and do

Top
  1. Sign in with the Google account your company registered. Your role is Vendor - <your company> (shown at the bottom left).
  2. Your menu: Dashboard, Masterlist, Vendor Truck Assignment, Scanning App, Fleet Management, Driver / Fleet Accounts and the guides.
  3. You only ever see your own trips, requests, trucks and drivers — never another vendor's, and never any rates or costs.

2. One-time set-up: trucks and drivers

Top
  1. Fleet Management → Add Truck: plate number, truck type, driver name and phone. The vendor is filled in for you.
  2. Driver / Fleet Accounts → Add Account: a username and password for the driver, their name and phone, the truck they drive and an Override code (4–32 letters / numbers).
  3. Give the driver their username and password — they use it in the Scanning App. Keep the override code with the supervisor: it is needed to accept a bag that is not on the manifest.
One account = one truck. The truck type decides which trips the account can be given.

3. Assign a driver to a trip

Top
  1. Open Vendor Truck Assignment. Each card is a trip waiting for a driver, with its final call time (or its Initial Call (TBC) if no final call is set yet), truck type and every drop. Use the Truck Type and Origin filters to find a trip quickly.
  2. In Select driver / fleet account, pick a driver. Only your active accounts whose truck matches the trip's truck type are listed (username, name, phone, plate).
  3. Click Assign Driver. The trip leaves this list and appears in the driver's Scanning App.
  4. No driver in the list? Add one (or set the right truck) on Driver / Fleet Accounts.
Wrong driver? On the Masterlist use Remove driver — the trip comes back here.

4. Follow your trips (Dashboard and Masterlist)

Top
  1. Dashboard: counts of your requests by status — click a card to list them.
  2. Masterlist: every request on your trips. Search or filter by Trip ID, MAWB, plate, status or dates.
  3. View shows all details, including the pickup / unloading scan manifest downloads once the driver has ended those scans, and the driver's Unloading Remarks for each drop.

5. Update a request (Edit)

Top
  1. On the Masterlist, click Edit.
  2. You can update the status, truck plate, Revised Qty, milestone times, foul trip details, attachments (e.g. proof of delivery) and the manifest.
  3. Greyed-out fields (account, department, ports, MAWBs, call times) belong to the requester and cannot be changed by vendors. A vendor also cannot move a request back to Pending.
Most milestone times are filled in automatically by the driver's scanning app — you rarely need to type them.

6. The Scanning App

Top
  1. Your drivers use the Scanning App on their phones — see the User Guide for Driver/Fleet, which drivers can also open with the Guide button inside the app.
  2. You can open it yourself from Scanning App in the menu to help a driver, signing in with that driver's account.

7. Problems and fixes

Top
  1. No driver to choose — the account is inactive, has no truck, or its truck type differs from the trip.
  2. A driver cannot sign in — check the username / password on Driver / Fleet Accounts (edit to set a new password) and that Account is active is ticked.
  3. A bag is not on the manifest — the requester must upload the right manifest, or the supervisor types the driver's override code to accept it.
  4. Anything else — note the message on screen and contact the SP PH operations team.

User Guide for Driver/Fleet

For drivers: how to use the Scanning App on your phone, from pickup to the last drop. Inside the app, tap Guide to open this page. Click a picture to open it full size.
1 Sign in

Username + password from your vendor.

2 Pickup

Arrive, scan the bags, end loading, depart.

3 Each drop

Arrive, scan bags off, add photos and remarks, finish.

Topics

1. Sign in
2. Work through the steps in order
3. Step 2 – Pickup scan (load the bags)
4. Steps 3 and 4 – End loading and Depart
5. At each drop
6. Finish the trip
7. Problems and fixes

1. Sign in

Top
  1. Open the app link on your phone (it ends in #/scan) — or tap Scanning App in the menu.
  2. Type the username and password your company gave you and tap Sign in. You stay signed in on this phone for 30 days.
  3. My trips lists the trips given to you. Tap Open trip.

2. Work through the steps in order

Top
  1. The trip shows numbered steps. A step only unlocks when the one before it is done (a lock shows what to do first).
  2. Work on one trip at a time — other trips stay locked until this one is finished.
  3. Step 1 – Arrived at pickup: tap it when the truck reaches the pickup point and confirm. If the trip has a final call time and you are past it, type the reason you are late.

3. Step 2 – Pickup scan (load the bags)

Top
  1. Tap Open pickup scan. Scan each bag with Scan with camera, or type the bag ID and tap Record bag. On a scanner device (e.g. Kaicom), just press the device's scan key. The scan key and Scan with camera cannot be used at the same time: tap Stop camera first (if you press the scan key while the camera is on, the app turns the camera off — press the scan key again).
  2. To clear a bag ID you typed or scanned, tap the at the right of the bag ID box — the whole ID is removed in one tap.
  3. Green + chime = bag accepted. Red + siren = not accepted (not on the manifest). Amber + siren = already scanned (not recorded twice).
  4. A bag not on the manifest can only be accepted if the supervisor types the override code and taps Override and record this bag.
  5. When every bag is loaded, tap End pickup scan, check the summary and tap Confirm (or Resume scanning if bags are missing). Manifest IDs that were never scanned are shown as (Discrepancy).
The camera reads each barcode once — hold the bag steady until you hear the chime. Another phone can help scan: it opens the Scan link QR code shown under the scan screen.

4. Steps 3 and 4 – End loading and Depart

Top
  1. Tap Back to the trip, then End loading when the truck is loaded.
  2. Tap Depart when you leave. The trip is now In Transit.

5. At each drop

Top
  1. Drops are done in order: Drop 1, then Drop 2, …
  2. (a) Arrived at destination — tap it when you arrive.
  3. (b) Unloading scan — tap Open unloading scan and scan each bag you unload. Only bags loaded for this drop are accepted (a bag accepted with the override code at pickup can be unloaded at any drop).
  4. Tap End Unloading Scan, check the summary and confirm. Bags still without an unloading scan are shown as (Discrepancy). It cannot be reopened afterwards.
  5. (c) Photos and remarks (optional) — tap Add photo to take or choose photos (e.g. proof of delivery), and type any Unloading remarks (e.g. damaged cartons, who received the bags).
  6. Check your photos — tap a photo to see it full screen (use the arrows for the others). To remove one, tap its (or Remove this photo) and confirm. Up to 8 files per drop; photos can be removed until you tap Finish Drop.
  7. (d) Finish Drop — tap it to complete the drop. Your remarks are saved with it and the next drop unlocks.

6. Finish the trip

Top
  1. Finishing the last drop completes the trip. It is marked Delivered and you can start your next trip from My trips.

7. Problems and fixes

Top
  1. Cannot sign in — check the username and password with your vendor; the account may have been switched off.
  2. No trip in My trips — your vendor has not assigned you yet.
  3. A step is locked — finish the step shown in the lock message first.
  4. Camera does not open — allow camera access for this site in your phone browser and close any other app using the camera (such as the device's own scanner app), then try again. On a scanner device (e.g. Kaicom), you can skip the camera and just press the scan key. You can always type the bag ID.
  5. Scan key does nothing — make sure the camera is stopped (the button shows Scan with camera). If it still does not read, close the browser tab, reopen the app link and try again.
  6. Red or amber + siren on a bag — read the message: red = not on the manifest (call your supervisor for the override code); amber = already scanned (no action needed).
